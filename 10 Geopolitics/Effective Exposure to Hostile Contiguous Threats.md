# Effective Exposure to Hostile Contiguous Threats

A better causal variable than simple land contiguity is **effective exposure to hostile contiguous military threats**.

```text
physical geography
+ alliance structure
+ relative power
+ technology
+ mobilization / force posture
+ institutional capacity
= effective exposure
```

This reframes maritime strategic position as a spectrum: the degree to which a state can defend its core territory primarily through control of maritime approaches rather than permanent preparation against major land-border threats.

This raises measurement questions: how to operationalize exposure, which dimensions belong in the construct versus causal model, and which conditions are definitions, moderators, or scope conditions.
