# Case Selection and Generalization

Case selection should match inferential purpose. Designs include positive/negative, near-miss, deviant/anomalous, most-likely, least-likely, most-similar, most-different, and typical/representative cases.

A future formalization could treat case selection as expected information gain over competing models.

**Idiographic** explanation targets one case in historical specificity. **Nomothetic** explanation formulates claims intended to travel across a class of cases. Establishing a mechanism in one case does not establish prevalence, necessity, sufficiency, or an average population effect.
