# Multiple Mechanisms and Rival Structures

Hypotheses need not be mutually exclusive whole stories. Relations can be competing, additive, conjunctural, synergistic, antagonistic/inhibitory, sequential, nested, equifinal, or substitutive.

Different hypotheses may reuse nodes, states, activities, or submechanisms. The important object is therefore a compositional causal structure rather than a flat list of mutually exclusive explanations.

Process tracing should ask not only which mechanism occurred, but which components occurred, how they interacted, and whether one mechanism changed the operation or evidentiary signature of another.
