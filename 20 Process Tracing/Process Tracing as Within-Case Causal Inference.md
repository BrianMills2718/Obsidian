# Process Tracing as Within-Case Causal Inference

Process tracing is primarily **within-case causal inference**: testing whether a hypothesized causal mechanism operated in a particular case by examining diagnostic evidence about intermediate causal processes.

A mechanism is causal, not merely a sequence. Candidate mechanisms should specify entities/states, activities or transition rules, temporal ordering, and observable implications. Rival explanations may reuse components of other hypotheses; they need not be reorderings of the same elements.

Theory testing should specify candidate mechanisms, counterfactual implications, observable traces, and evidence-update logic before evidence collection where possible. Theory building can iterate when anomalies motivate new hypotheses.
