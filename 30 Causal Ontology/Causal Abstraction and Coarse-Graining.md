# Causal Abstraction and Coarse-Graining

The question "how far should a mechanism be decomposed?" can be reframed as causal abstraction.

```text
neural/cognitive microstates -> coarse-grained belief state -> decision -> action
```

Candidate stopping principle: **stop at the coarsest causal representation that preserves the distinctions, interventions, and counterfactual queries required by the research problem.**

Open questions: when levels are causally equivalent for a query set; what information may be discarded; when aggregation creates spurious causal structure; whether macro-variables support well-defined interventions; and how to use evidence finer-grained than the tested mechanism.
