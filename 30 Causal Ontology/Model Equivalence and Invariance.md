# Model Equivalence and Invariance

Use query-relative equivalence:
```text
M1 ~_Q M2
```
Two models are equivalent relative to query set Q if they preserve the answers relevant to Q. A DAG, structural equations, state-transition system, and textual mechanism description can preserve different subsets of meaning.

Counterfactual reasoning also requires explicit invariance assumptions: what remains stable when X is changed? Candidates include structural equations, actor decision rules, preferences, institutions, measurement processes, source-production mechanisms, and background conditions.

This may make invariance more fundamental than generic scope-condition language.
