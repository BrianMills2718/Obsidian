# Measurement Theory

Measurement theory bridges ontology and evidence:
```text
ontology -> measurement theory -> observation/trace production -> evidence -> inference
```

Example latent construct: **perceived invasion risk**. Indicators might include private threat language, intelligence assessments, readiness decisions, deployments, cabinet minutes, and later testimony. The indicator is not the construct itself.

Distinguish causal heterogeneity (`P(Y | do(X), context)` changes) from measurement heterogeneity (`P(O | X, context)` changes). A bureaucracy may systematically exaggerate threats in documents, changing the observation model even when the underlying causal process is unchanged.
