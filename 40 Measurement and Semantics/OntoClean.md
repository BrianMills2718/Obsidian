# OntoClean

OntoClean evaluates whether a taxonomy is ontologically coherent using meta-properties including **rigidity**, **identity**, **unity**, and **dependence**.

Example: Person is plausibly a rigid type; Student, leader, employee, president, and prisoner are anti-rigid roles.

Questions for process tracing: Is Leader a type or role? Is Evidence an entity or a role relative to a claim? Does Belief depend on an agent as bearer? Is Mechanism a process instance or reusable type? Is Current intrinsic or time-indexed? Is Gap an entity or a relation between target and realized state?
